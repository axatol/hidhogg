Use case logo-brand. Professional logo exploration board for HIDHogg, native macOS app controlling multiple input devices. This round explores HEDGEHOG motifs, not dragons. Landscape clean warm white board, four DISTINCT concepts in a 2 by 2 grid, generous whitespace. Exact heading HIDHogg — hedgehog directions. Each concept has a large original mark, small rounded-square macOS app icon study and small monochrome silhouette study. Restrained memorable graphic design, simple bold vector-like shapes rendered as raster. A label A · Spine hub: elegant side-profile hedgehog with 5 broad geometric spines radiating from one compact body, dark navy and cobalt accent, minimal eye and pointed snout, no thin lines. Spines subtly express several devices one controller, no literal device symbols. B label B · Hidden H: abstract compact hedgehog silhouette using negative space to form an H in body, charcoal and teal, refined recognizable silhouette, 5-7 clear spines, no wordmark disguised as spikes. C label C · Companion: friendly mature front-facing hedgehog bust, simple face and broad spines, slate and warm orange, not baby toy, not sonic, not game character, no hands or clothing. D label D · Curled connection: geometric hedgehog curled into a near-circle, head peeking at lower right, broad spines around upper arc, bold connected shape in deep blue and pale blue, strong small-size readability. Under each main mark HIDHogg wordmark in clean sans-serif. Avoid dragons, multi heads, cables, USB marks, literal mouse/keyboard pictograms, esports shields, hyperdetail, photographic textures, elaborate gradients, excessive spikes, existing mascot likeness.
